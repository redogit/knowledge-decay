## Question
Can a relation-aware record preserve the answer to "must B finish before A?" where a parts-only record cannot, while refusing an answer when the required relation is missing or stale?

## Frozen small model
Use two worlds with the same parts A and B and the same part contents. In one, A is independent; in the other, A requires B's output. The world revision and the dependency are explicit. Do not infer human or universal system benefit from this model.

## Comparators
1. A parts-only inventory.
2. An otherwise matched record containing the correct dependency and whole-state revision.
3. The relation-aware record with its dependency removed, contradictory, or stale.

## Predetermined outcomes
- **Supported in this model:** the parts-only observation is identical across worlds; the valid relation-aware record distinguishes the required answer. Save the exact model, outputs, and costs; only propose a bounded finding.
- **Counterexample or contradiction:** retain the failing case and revise the interpretation or record contract. Do not erase the earlier result.
- **Missing/stale/insufficient evidence:** return UNRESOLVED or require a justified refresh; do not fabricate a dependency or final NO.
- **Stop:** after these finite cases, record the result and remaining obligations. Do not expand to a general knowledge system without a new plan.
