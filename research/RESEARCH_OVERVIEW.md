# Research overview

Knowledge Decay investigates how knowledge can remain understandable, discoverable, and useful as its context changes. It treats incomplete evidence as a starting point for careful reconstruction and new experiments, while distinguishing recovered understanding from newly developed explanations.

The current research focuses on the relationship between individual records and the larger systems they describe. Each record should have a stable identity, traceable revisions, explicit dependencies, and a clear account of its scope and uncertainty. Shared conventions should support coordination without making every participant or record identical.

The proposed method combines historical research, versioned information structures, and bounded experiments. Its purpose is to help people explore unfamiliar perspectives, evaluate competing explanations, and retain what is needed to revisit earlier reasoning.

This repository is a research foundation. It does not yet establish a deployed autonomous system, complete reconstruction of lost knowledge, or general improvements in human learning.
