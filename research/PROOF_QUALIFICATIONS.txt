PROOF-REVIEW QUALIFICATIONS — SOURCE TEXTS RETAINED UNCHANGED

These are this review's mathematical qualifications, not claims that the original
source has been edited or that all proof obligations have been audited.

1. MP-001 and MP-058: decoder domain.
The proof defines a decoder on the image of the carrier. Use d:P(X)->Z.
A total decoder on unused carrier values additionally needs an available value
in Z. No algorithmic efficiency or human intelligibility follows from existence.

2. MP-036 / MP-064: safe actions and randomization.
The deterministic common-action criterion is sound. The randomized support
argument is safe for finite/discrete action models. Without qualifications it
need not extend to uncountable action spaces with pointwise almost-sure safety:
each state x could forbid just action x in [0,1]; uniform random action violates
no fixed state's rule with positive probability, although the intersection of
all allowed-action sets is empty. Fix the intended safety quantifiers.

3. MP-070: retained history matters.
Identical future observations do not erase an earlier observation that already
resolved the target. Apply the claim only to candidates compatible with the same
complete retained transcript. Otherwise a remembered earlier answer is a direct
counterexample to the unqualified assertion.

4. MP-074: obtainability of the joint experiment vector.
Individually available tests may be mutually incompatible in an irreversible
world. The factorization proof is correct for the joint vector, but operational
discovery also requires that the vector can be obtained from the required common
context or via another justified protocol.

5. MP-075: define success.
The source's at-most-1/2 bound proves a limit on choosing the diagnostic experiment
without using the cue. It does not automatically bound accuracy when guessing the
hidden bit is allowed. With uniformly random bit, p=1/2 diagnostic access and fair
guessing otherwise gives (1/2)*1+(1/2)*(1/2)=3/4 accuracy. The contextual strategy
still guarantees diagnostic access in the stated family. Preserve that claim,
not an overstated probability bound.

6. PIVOT lifecycle source: status declarations are not demonstrations.
Its reported user requests are useful provenance. Broad assistant OUTPUT claims,
including the final 'Q.E.D.' status, do not supply mathematical proofs, biological
validation, language universality, or verified production implementation.

7. Grouping and identity.
Closeness, agreement, a shared group, identical text, or a shared identifier format
cannot themselves establish evidence independence or semantic identity. The
historical Chained-object identifier and current Change-object wording remain
separately recorded until the user establishes their intended relation.
