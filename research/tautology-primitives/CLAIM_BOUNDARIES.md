# Claim boundaries

## Established in this module

### Binary single-connective TRUE generation
For a binary connective `g`, a tautology using only `g` and variables exists iff `g` is one of NOR, XNOR, reverse implication, implication, NAND, or binary constant TRUE. Identifying every variable with `x` reduces the existence question to a unary closure contained in `{0,1,x,NOT x}`.

### Binary-basis TRUE generation
For sets of binary Boolean operators, TRUE is generable iff the basis is neither wholly 0-preserving nor wholly self-dual. Exact enumeration covers every singleton and pair drawn from the complete sixteen binary operations, and every escaping larger binary basis contains an escaping singleton or pair.

### State-cycle software contract
The implementation preserves all 53 carrier bits across float-bit transport, preserves all 46 non-word coordinate bits through the cycle, implements `w' = 2w+1 mod 128`, and rejects the recorded lifecycle mutations.

## Not established

- no polynomial-time general tautology recognizer;
- no SAT lower bound;
- no P=NP or P!=NP result;
- no proof that DPLL is polynomial on arbitrary inputs;
- no arbitrary-arity analogue of the two-obstruction binary-basis theorem;
- no direct Episteme runtime compatibility;
- no transfer of evidence merely because the architecture resembles Episteme.
