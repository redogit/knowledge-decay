## recent.identity — Identity across changes of viewpoint
A declared identity policy can keep an object stable while its coordinates or presentation change. This is a scoped design rule, not a universal identity theorem.

## OP-BOUND-055 — Identity during cooperation
Cooperating components retain their separate identities. The record establishes a design distinction; implementation evidence must be assessed separately.

## OP-BOUND-106 — Cooperation and authority
A connection between components does not automatically transfer decision authority. This is a source-declared boundary, not a verified deployment result.

## OP-BOUND-124 — Local and system-level outcomes
Success in one component does not establish success of the larger system. Whole-system requirements need their own evaluation.

## CL-00010 — Contextual preservation
Recovery requires more than retained files: objectives, assumptions, chronology, applicability, uncertainty, and unsuccessful attempts may be necessary to interpret them. This is an adopted research method, not an established empirical benefit.

## CL-00026 — Object identifiers and content hashes
The recorded design assigns different functions to persistent 16-byte identifiers, epoch-scoped 8-byte handles, revisions, and content hashes. It also separates collection time from event time. These are declared contracts, not newly verified guarantees.

## CL-01033 — Task-relative context
The earlier definition treats context as the information needed to interpret a representation for a particular task, considering reconstruction cost. Its practical sufficiency requires evaluation.

## CL-01034 — Task obligations
An obligation specifies a condition that must hold, be preserved, or be adequately resolved for a task to succeed. This is a recorded definition rather than an experimental result.

## CL-00341 — Task sufficiency without full disclosure
In the stated partition model, determining a target can require less information than identifying the complete underlying state. The corollary remains conditional on that model and target.

## CL-00348 — Redundant reports
Reports derived deterministically from one retained report need not add any ability to distinguish underlying cases, even when their source identifiers differ. This is a model-scoped corollary, not a universal statement about all multisource evidence.

## CL-00310 — Content and object identity
Distinct objects can contain the same bytes. The source records a model witness separating content equality from object identity; the witness has not been rerun here.

## CL-00311 — Content and admission status
The custody model permits identical content to have different review or admission states. Changing that state is a separate governed event. The historical result is not new verification.

## CL-00313 — Semantic contracts across representations
The recorded experiment reports failures involving ordering and null-versus-empty values during an untyped conversion, with an explicit adapter supplying the missing distinctions. This remains a historical system report, not a rerun result.

## EXTRA-FOLLOW-R06 — Preservation and recovery operations
The specification separates provenance, chronology, integrity, evidence limits, retained failures, reconstruction material, lineage, aliases, deprecation, and revival. Naming these operations does not establish an implemented lifecycle.
