# Public material and source availability

The current repository contains edited project documentation, research indexes, scoped mathematical qualifications, and hypothetical examples. It does not publish the complete archival source corpus or private working conversations.

## Editorial material

The research overview, integrated summary, requirement map, and related-record summaries are public-facing explanations. They are not verbatim source transcripts. Source identifiers and applicable evidence limits are retained without treating access to private material as permission to publish it.

## Archival references

SOURCE_IMPORT.json records the original packet members, their digests, and their current publication dispositions. The original packet and selected research companion remain outside this repository. Their availability must not be confused with public accessibility, and hashes do not substitute for source bytes.

The mathematical qualifications and two hypothetical examples remain unchanged from the recorded source packet. Other project prose has been edited. Initialization receipts and original-source checks describe the earlier snapshot only; their counts and byte-identity claims do not apply to later edited documents.

## Review limits

The recovered proof ledger is incomplete, and the standalone Stairs of State specification remains unavailable in this corpus. Editorial revision does not close those gaps, certify proofs, execute experiments, or establish human benefit.

The current-branch revision does not erase prior commits or copies outside this repository. See ../PUBLICATION_POLICY.md for publication permissions and review requirements.
