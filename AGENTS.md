# Repository-scoped instructions

Read README.md, CHARTER.md, PUBLICATION_POLICY.md, research/PROOF_QUALIFICATIONS.txt, and NEXT_STEP.md before making changes.

- Write for a public research audience. Extract the intended ideas and express them clearly; do not reproduce informal conversations or personal disclosures.
- Raw conversations and working notes are private by default. Access, preservation, and provenance do not confer permission to publish them. Do not copy them into documentation, issues, pull requests, commit messages, fixtures, or public logs without explicit publication authorization.
- Preserve the human learning purpose. Technical models are instruments, not the entire definition of Knowledge Decay.
- Retain the distinctions among source claims, current review, hypotheses, proofs, experiments, and unverified applications. Editorial changes do not strengthen the evidence.
- Keep object identity, occurrence, revision, representation, interpretation, membership, and authority separate.
- Do not equate Chained and Change identifiers or invent the missing Stairs of State specification.
- Do not replace whole-group obligations with pairwise checks or treat unknown membership as exclusion.
- Define each action's inputs, scope, budget, outcome-dependent successors, stop condition, and recovery route before execution. Record actual outcomes only.
- Preserve authorized archival evidence separately from edited public summaries. Keep chronology, attribution, assumptions, counterexamples, and qualifications; privacy takes precedence over reproducing source wording on a public surface.
- Reconcile provenance/SOURCE_IMPORT.json when an archival copy becomes an editorial summary. A hash does not make an unavailable source accessible.
- Do not publish private archives, unrelated personal data, secrets, or font binaries. Do not modify another repository or run an experiment merely because it is referenced here.
- Editing the current branch does not erase prior commits or external copies. History rewrites and repository visibility changes require explicit authorization.
- Verify changed documents, references, structured data, and remote writes. Distinguish document integrity, software behavior, mathematical correctness, and human benefit.
