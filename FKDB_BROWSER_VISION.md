# FKDB — A Browser for Knowledge Exploration and Preservation

## Purpose

FKDB is a browser designed to support people who gather knowledge. Its purpose is to make discovering, collecting, connecting, investigating, preserving, and applying information more accessible while retaining the context necessary to understand it.

This document describes the intended direction of the project, not a claim that all features are implemented or formally proved.

## Core commitments

- **Identity and provenance:** Distinguish source, capture, observation, claim, interpretation, revision, and execution. Preserve where information came from, how it was found, and why it was sought.
- **Recoverable history:** Retain exact originals or explicitly document recovery limits, historical versions, dependency relationships, and the route back to source evidence.
- **Knowledge development:** Support patterns, competing explanations, counterexamples, and assumption-first investigation of unexpected results.
- **Contextual justification:** Detect when a source, assumption, or context changes; selectively recheck dependent claims. Stale or unsupported is not synonymous with false.
- **Human-centered navigation:** Present useful summaries with direct access to originals, supporting evidence, uncertainty, and practical next actions.
- **Decentralized preservation:** Explore independent storage and verification of versioned evidence without equating repeated copies with independent support or granting unapproved sharing authority.
- **Composable tools:** Explore typed entities, verified transformations, reusable arithmetic and Boolean circuits, residual-guided planning, and witness-based allocation as supporting mechanisms, not prerequisites for browsing.

## Proposed FKDB workflow

NEED → DISCOVER → CAPTURE → PRESERVE → RELATE → CHECK → RETURN → ACT → REVISIT

Each transition retains an identifiable purpose, source/version references, declared context, and any outstanding validation obligations.

## Knowledge decay as a supported capability

Knowledge decay may concern unavailable sources, changed content, lost provenance, outdated context, invalidated assumptions, or weakened justification. FKDB should distinguish these conditions and preserve prior evidence. A changed page does not automatically refute a claim; a copied page does not create independent corroboration.

## Verification and boundaries

Research targets include exact recovery under a stated failure model; dependency-aware revalidation without missed affected claims under a complete dependency graph; conflict-safe decentralized updates; and measured retrieval and verification costs. These remain proof and experimental obligations until demonstrated.

Do not silently ingest or publish private information. Preserve existing repository publication rules and distinguish proposed architecture from implemented behavior. No patentability, polynomial-time general solver, or autonomous deployment claim is made.

## Intended benefit

Help people explore what is known, understand how it became known, discover what remains unknown, and continue learning—with the fewest necessary actions and a faithful route back to the underlying evidence.
